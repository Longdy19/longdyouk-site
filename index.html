About Longdy

From Engineering Structures to Designing Business Systems

My journey began in civil engineering and construction, then expanded through service engineering and strategic management. A decade of international AEC experience showed me how strong ideas can lose momentum when leadership, operations, and delivery become disconnected.

That insight shaped my work in business development and systems thinking — and now informs the ventures I build. Longdinovat applies the approach to organizational systems; Anijak brings an engineering and development perspective to projects.

The philosophy

The Coherent Systems Model™

A practical way to connect direction, organizational capability, delivery, and leadership into one coherent system.

01 / DIRECTION

Strategy

Determines where the organization is going and what matters most.

02 / CAPABILITY

Operations

Creates the processes, structures, and capabilities to move forward.

03 / DELIVERY

Execution

Turns strategic intent into coordinated action and results.

04 / COHERENCE

Leadership

Keeps people, decisions, and the wider system aligned.

This framework informs how I advise organizations, build Longdinovat, and develop Anijak.
